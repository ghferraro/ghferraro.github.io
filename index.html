I use economic methods to study agricultural, environmental, and health topics. More specifically, I study how agricultural investment decisions interact with "exogenous" enviromental and health systems, as well as how chemical exposures affect human health. I am interested in how observable human decisions elucidate hard-to-otherwise-observe environmental states and how humans react to policy interventions. I also have experience in social and international development having been a Peace Corps Volunteer, an NGO/non-profit employee, and a Fulbright scholar. I'd like to look back on my life knowing I meaningly contributed to domestic and international rural and social development.
